# Code of Conduct

OpenKrishi AI is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive open-source community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome different perspectives and backgrounds.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Help keep discussions useful and safe for contributors and farmers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, or other harmful conduct is not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, open a private contact channel with the project maintainers when available. For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` instead.
