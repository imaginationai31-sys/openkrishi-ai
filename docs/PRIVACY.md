# OpenKrishi AI — Privacy

## Principles
Collect the minimum data required to provide the service.

## Data Categories
Potential data includes:
- account identity
- language and crop preferences
- advisory requests
- crop images
- voice/audio data
- approximate/location data for weather where explicitly enabled
- feedback
- operational telemetry

## Purpose Limitation
Use each category only for stated product functionality, safety, security, reliability, or legally required purposes.

## User Controls
Provide mechanisms to view, delete, or manage applicable user data according to the product's legal and operational requirements.

## Sensitive Content
Crop images, audio, location, and user questions may reveal personal or farm information. Restrict access and avoid unnecessary retention.

## Third Parties
Document AI, storage, authentication, analytics, and infrastructure providers that process user data. Provider contracts and applicable legal requirements must be reviewed before production launch.

## Security
Encryption in transit and at rest should be enabled where supported. Access follows least privilege and is auditable.

## Privacy by Design
New features must identify collected data, purpose, retention, access controls, and deletion behavior before release.
